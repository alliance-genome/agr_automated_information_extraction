# ATP:0000070 regulatory interaction (gene regulation)

**Status:** draft, not yet reviewed by WB curators.

Positive: the paper shows that a genetic perturbation (mutation, RNAi, overexpression) or an environmental or
chemical treatment changes the expression level or localization of a specific C. elegans gene or gene product
(reporter, qRT-PCR, Western, antibody staining), or that a specific regulator directly controls a specific target
(e.g. a transcription factor binding and regulating a target gene's promoter).
Not positive:
- wild-type expression patterns only (that is ATP:0000041);
- genetic or physical interactions without an expression or localization readout;
- reporter changes that only reflect loss of the cells that express it, or markers used to count cells;
- large-scale profiling only without gene-specific follow-up; general effects on transcription.
